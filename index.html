Our Story

Born from necessity.
Driven by compassion.

In many parts of Kenya, accessing basic healthcare is a luxury. Preventable diseases claim lives daily due to distance, cost, or lack of resources. Dalla Community Hub was established to intercept this cycle.

We operate directly within informal settlements and remote villages, establishing mobile clinics and training local community health workers. By empowering communities from the inside out, we ensure that care is not just delivered, but sustained.

The Reality.

Millions in rural and peri-urban Kenya lack access to basic clinical facilities. Treatable conditions escalate to emergencies.

Our Intervention.

Fully-equipped mobile medical clinics deployed directly to vulnerable populations — removing distance as a barrier.

Long-Term Impact.

Training Community Health Volunteers for continuous education — creating sustainable, self-reliant communities.

Core Initiatives.

Comprehensive solutions tailored to the specific needs of the communities we serve.

Mobile Outreach

Fully staffed mobile clinics traveling to remote areas — general consultations, medicines, emergency referrals.

Maternal Health

Pre and post-natal care focused on reducing maternal mortality. Nutritional supplements and safe delivery kits.

Community Education

Training local volunteers in hygiene, sanitation, and disease prevention for long-term resilience.
